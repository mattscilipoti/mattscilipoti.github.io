# Who Am I?

I'm Matt Scilipoti

I code. 

I am not _just_ a coder.    
I am not a (ninja|rockstar|10xer).

I am comfortable with, and encourage, [DevOps](https://12factor.net/).

I love this stuff.

I am a perfectly imperfect human.     
I can do it alone, but you really want me as a member of a team.
